# Invariants — Gauges, states, modes, chain nodes, rotation

Breaking any one silently corrupts sim output. Code comments cite these as
`CLAUDE.md, "<title>"`, so keep the titles stable.

| Invariant | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Cast triggers are MECHANICAL | `castMatch` trigger firing reads the node `skillType` only (`phraseTypesForStep` in sim.js), never `formulaType` — a Basic that deals converted Liberation damage is still a Basic CAST |
| Gauge caps come from the GAME | `SpecialEnergy{N}Max` (BinData `baseproperty.json` → `extract-forte.mjs` → `resonator.specialEnergyCaps`) is authoritative for how many stacks a gauge holds. A curated `RESOURCE_DEFS` entry declares its `channel` and its literal `cap` is test-asserted equal — but a channel is CLAIMED, never guessed: Luuk Herssen's Endnotes on the Endgame holds 3 and none of his five channels reads 3, while TWO of them reach it under a divisor (ch2 at unit 100, ch3 at unit 50) and `gauge-income.json` carries no SpecialEnergy row of his to settle which. With no second witness the entry declares no channel and takes the cap from the kit, which states it twice independently. An unclaimed channel understates provenance; a wrong one silently rescales a gauge |
| A CHAIN NODE CAN ADD A SPENDER, and gauge income is a CAST not a BUCKET | `chainOverrides` merges cap/start/tick AND `spendAll`/`spend`; a spender list is REPLACED rather than merged, because it is a complete statement of who drains a gauge. Galbrena's Afterflame needs it: only her S6 says Ascent of Malice consumes it ("for every 1 point of Afterflame consumed"), while her base kit's own uses read the level HELD, so an unconditional spend would silently zero them. **THE INCOME IS A MECHANICAL CAST, NOT AN ATTRIBUTION.** "Galbrena recovers 8 points of [Afterflame] when Resonators in the nearby team cast Echo Skill" — her Basic Attack Stage 4 DEALS Echo Skill DMG (`dmgTypes: ["echo"]`) and grants nothing, because the bucket is not a cast; solo she gains 8, not 16 (maintainer, 2026-10-04). Kits can convert a CAST (e.g. Lucilla's "Each cast of [Oblivion] is considered as casting a different Echo Skill", Roccia's "considered Echo Skill") and the synthetic `__echo__` step is a legal `gains` key even though it has no `autoSkillMap` entry. UNDERSTATES BY CONSTRUCTION: the income is ANY member's cast while `rotation-resources.js` walks one member's own rotation: Galbrena's Afterflame counts only her own echo cast, and Sigrika's Soliskin Vitality, fed the same way, has no gauge until team-wide gauge income exists. History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |
| A gauge does not necessarily START empty, and a chain node moves start and cap TOGETHER | A fight opens a gauge on `RESOURCE_DEFS.start` (else 0), because a kit can restore a gauge on entering combat: Denia's inherent Vestiges of Falsehood — "restore Dark Cores to 2 if she has fewer than 2" — feeds her Banish Stage 2's "+150% per Dark Core consumed". `chainOverrides` is the per-chain edit: S3 raises her limit to 5 AND enhances the inherent to "restored to the max", so the two must move together — an S3 build opens 5/5 and its Intro grant overflows, while at S0 the same grant is needed. A CARRIED level OUTRANKS `start` (`startLevels?.get(name) ?? def.start ?? 0` in `walkResource`), because `start` is what entering the FIGHT gives, not each segment: re-reading it per segment refills the gauge for free. Only the S3 branch is in the data (trigger row `1211700101`, cd 12.0, +100% of `SpecialEnergy2Max`); the base floor and the 3→5 bump appear nowhere in `gauge-income.json` — no `isCap` row exists for her — so those two numbers are the kit's, validated by `tests/rotation-resources.test.mjs` reproducing the game's own pre-multiplied THREE-core row. It is a CYCLE — see the tick invariant below. History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |
| A gauge tick is a CLOCK, and the gate withholds the EFFECT not the CLOCK | "Once every 12s" rate-limits a passive that re-checks its condition; not a one-shot: `RESOURCE_DEFS.tick` fires at elapsed 0 and every `period` after, on **gameTime** (frozen in Liberation animations). `refillTo` RAISES to a floor and never lowers; `amount` is the additive shape; `refillTo: 'cap'` names the gauge's own limit, so a chain node's cap raise lifts the floor too. A FORM decides how far a firing refills, NOT whether it fires: `byState` varies the VALUE while the count of firings still advances, so a lower value cannot delay the next firing. Denia's passive stops at 2 Dark Cores in Stagecraft and is unlimited in Breakdown, under a CAP of 3 in both (maintainer, 2026-09-02; only the Stagecraft half is in kit text, neither in `gauge-income.json`). A firing landing exactly on step i's start reads step i-1's states; the t=0 firing is in step 0's entering level. The phase CARRIES across segments (`resourceEndTickPhases` -> `carryInResourceTicks`) because a tick belongs to the FIGHT: restarted per segment it re-fires its opening tick on every swap-in, free income. Counting firings (`floor(T / period) + 1`) keeps that carry exact. No `stepTimes` (rotation-graph legality) means an INERT clock, never a guessed schedule. Denia's two 12s rows are the only wired case; only unit tests exercise the cycle (her reference run is under one period). History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |
| A chain node can CREATE the skill a rotation step names | Lupa S6: "Forte Circuit Dance With the Wolf is replaced with Dance With the Wolf: Climax". The game ships BOTH as damage rows (12070007001, 12070007002), so both resolve as ordinary skill keys and an S0 build could cast the S6 one — her own REFERENCE ROTATION does, and `team-rank.js` builds members at chain 0 by default. `CHAIN_LOCKED_SKILLS` + `resolveChainLockedRotation` SUBSTITUTE rather than drop, because the player presses the same input either way and the game decides which move comes out; dropping the step would understate by removing a cast that happens. The pair is reported as `chainLockedSubstitutions` rather than applied silently. A lock is only expressible when BOTH keys exist — Qiuyuan S3's Straw Cape in Drizzly Rain and Camellya S6's Perennial have NO rows at all, so those are missing-damage gaps (understating) and cannot be written as locks. History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |
| Gauge income is readable ON A CAST, and only there | Where a gauge's income is not on its damage instances (Changli's 40 all read `SpecialEnergy 0`), it is on the CAST: `DT_SkillInfo` row → `SkillBuff`/`SkillStartBuff`/`SkillEndBuff` → `db_buff.GameAttributeID` ∈ `Proto_(Special)Energy*` (`data/gauge-income.json` ← `extract_gauge_income.py`). What it CANNOT see is income earned on a HIT: that lives in `db_PassiveSkill` (`DamageTrigger`, with its own CD) behind ExtraEffect chains, which is why Changli's Enflamement and Camellya's Crimson Bud show nothing in the cast lane and stay curated. The trigger lane is extracted but NOT wired — `rotation-resources.js` is per-cast by construction. Read the magnitude through `CalculationPolicy` (`ActiveBuff.ModifyStateAttribute`): **0** flat add, **1** scale base (`-10000` ⇒ zeroed), **2/4/9** fraction of `policy[1]`, **3** override (`0` ⇒ zeroed). Draining a gauge by its own full size is a flat `add` of `-cap`, NOT a `spendAll` shape — a reader that looks only for `spendAll` misses half the roster's spends. History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |
| A Resonance MODE is not a STATE | A mode is a build-level toggle, locked for the fight (`build.resonanceMode` → `effect.mode` → `modeGateOk`). `STATE_DEFS` is for in-combat states a CAST enters. Modelling a mode as a state makes it switch on mid-rotation and ignore the build's own toggle — and the state name can only ever match one mode, so it fires in the other one too. A roster-wide test forbids a state named after a mode |
| A missing STATE is not one missing clause, and an aggregate is not a cause | A kit gated on a named state resolves OFF entirely without a `STATE_DEFS` entry, so not modelling one costs EVERY clause that names it — and the total then looks like a single effect's worth. DECOMPOSE an aggregate, stripping each effect in turn, before naming a cause. Galbrena's [Demon Hypostasis]: the ENTRY is derived; her text never states it: her S1 node grants, "when casting Resonance Skill - Ascent of Malice", a buff "removed upon exiting Demon Hypostasis", and a cast whose grant dies with the state starts it. The Liberation is NOT an entry though it carries SN0.0's +85% DMG Multiplier on the Forte moves her node names (Purgatory Scourge has no row) — its own node says "While in [Demon Hypostasis], cast [Seraphic Execution Stage 2] instead", presupposing the state. EXIT takes the kit's 50s rather than `persist`; the real limiter is Purging Flame depleting, unmodelled, so 50s is the longer bound, overstating an early end — it cannot bite while her rotation re-enters the state every team cycle. A clause stating BOTH a duration and a state ("for 14s while in [Demon Hypostasis]") keeps only the state half in the parser, a measured non-difference while every post-Liberation step lands within 14s and she re-casts each team cycle. In her shipped rotation Afterflame never exceeds one echo cast's worth (`clearedOnSwap` plus one member's income). History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |
| The game may ship a per-resource multiplier as PRE-MULTIPLIED rows — read them as the answer key, not as the model | Denia's [Banish - Breakdown Form Stage 2] displays 56.34%, and `damageTable` also holds `12111052110/…120/…130/…140/…150` at exactly 2.5× / 4.0× / 5.5× / 7.0× / 8.5× of it — `base × (1 + 1.5N)` for N = 1..5 Dark Cores (five because S3 states "Denia now holds up to 5"). The skill's own row (`12110002005`) IS present and IS what the sim uses, so those variants are not needed to make the cast deal damage; attaching them as extra hits would double-count. They are the CHECK: `computeDamage` already composes `baseMult × (1 + multiplierUp)`, so a correct per-resource model must reproduce each variant exactly, and `tests/rotation-resources.test.mjs` asserts all five at every level of the curve |
| A "for each [X] CONSUMED" multiplier scopes ITSELF | The clause names no skill ("the DMG Multiplier of the attack"), so there is no name to bind — and none is needed. Its stack count reads what the STEP SPENDS (`computeResourceConsumption`, `stackTrigger.consumed`), which is 0 on every cast that spends nothing, so the effect is scoped by arithmetic and is exempt from the unscoped-`multiplierUp` drop. Two things make that safe and must stay true: the window is `thisCast` (with `persist` the effect is dark on the very cast it is for unless a sibling key fired first, then live on every step after), and an UNCURATED gauge consumes 0 rather than reading as unknown — `resourceConsumedAt` returns 0, never null, so a gauge the app does not model understates instead of paying on every cast. A clause scaling on the gauge merely HELD is NOT this shape and still needs a real scope |
| A gauge belongs to the CHARACTER, not to a segment | A team turn is simulated as several `simulateRotation` calls — the auto-injected Intro is its own segment — so a gauge earned in one and spent in another reads empty unless it is carried. `simulateRotation` takes `carryInResources` and returns `resourceEndLevels`; team-sim threads them through `sim.memberResources`, Intro → rotation → next team cycle, exactly as `memberFires` carries the trigger ledger. Denia earns her Dark Core on Intro and spends it on Banish Stage 2, so without the carry her multiplier is correct solo and silently zero in every team sim |
| A fight starts on a FULL Resonance meter, and a curated rotation is performed AS AUTHORED | Maintainer-directed 2026-08-14, modelled on the Tower of Adversity start state (`team-energy.js`, `team-sim.js`: "STARTS FULL"). Resonance Energy starts FULL (`baseStats.energyMax`) for every resonator; Concerto and Forte/SpecialEnergy start EMPTY; overcap is impossible (the gauge clamps at the cost, and the cost IS the meter). Generation spilled before the first Liberation is fine: a Liberation is placed for its BUFF WINDOW, not its energy efficiency. The engine may neither splice filler into a curated rotation nor drop a cast from it; a short gauge is a BUILD problem, so report the shortfall and the ER that fixes it. Team cycle 1 is the WEAKEST team cycle (empty Concerto, no buffs ramped); the ER target binds from the SECOND team cycle. History: `docs/history/rules/invariants-resources.md` |

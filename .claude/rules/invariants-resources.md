# Invariants — Gauges, states, modes, chain nodes, rotation

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-05 (see its INSTRUCTION FILES section).
Breaking any one silently corrupts sim output. Code comments cite these as
`CLAUDE.md, "<title>"`; the titles are unchanged.

| Invariant | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Cast triggers are MECHANICAL | `castMatch` trigger firing reads the node `skillType` only (`phraseTypesForStep` in sim.js), never `formulaType` — a Basic that deals converted Liberation damage is still a Basic CAST |
| Gauge caps come from the GAME | `SpecialEnergy{N}Max` (BinData `baseproperty.json` → `extract-forte.mjs` → `resonator.specialEnergyCaps`) is authoritative for how many stacks a gauge holds. A curated `RESOURCE_DEFS` entry declares its `channel` and its literal `cap` is test-asserted equal — but a channel is CLAIMED, never guessed: Luuk Herssen's Endnotes on the Endgame holds 3 and none of his five channels reads 3, while TWO of them reach it under a divisor (ch2 at unit 100, ch3 at unit 50) and `gauge-income.json` carries no SpecialEnergy row of his to settle which. With no second witness the entry declares no channel and takes the cap from the kit, which states it twice independently. An unclaimed channel understates provenance; a wrong one silently rescales a gauge |
| A CHAIN NODE CAN ADD A SPENDER, and gauge income is a CAST not a BUCKET | `chainOverrides` merged only cap/start/tick, so a curated chain-gated `spendAll` was SILENTLY IGNORED — the entry looked right and did nothing. It now merges `spendAll`/`spend` too, REPLACED rather than merged, because a spender list is a complete statement of who drains a gauge. Galbrena's Afterflame needs it: only her S6 says Ascent of Malice consumes it (\"for every 1 point of Afterflame consumed\"), while her base kit's own uses read the level HELD, so an unconditional spend would silently zero them. **THE INCOME IS A MECHANICAL CAST, NOT AN ATTRIBUTION.** \"Galbrena recovers 8 points of [Afterflame] when Resonators in the nearby team cast Echo Skill\" — her Basic Attack Stage 4 DEALS Echo Skill DMG (`dmgTypes: [\"echo\"]`) and grants nothing, because the bucket is not a cast; solo she gains 8, not 16 (maintainer, 2026-10-04). Exactly 2 kits convert a CAST (Lucilla's \"Each cast of [Oblivion] is considered as casting a different Echo Skill\", Roccia's \"considered Echo Skill\") and the synthetic `__echo__` step is a legal `gains` key even though it has no `autoSkillMap` entry. UNDERSTATES BY CONSTRUCTION: the income is ANY member's cast while `rotation-resources.js` walks one member's own rotation, so Galbrena reads 8 of 40 and Sigrika's Soliskin Vitality never reaches its 30 threshold — team-wide gauge income is one feature with two concrete consumers |
| A gauge does not necessarily START empty, and a chain node moves start and cap TOGETHER | `walkResource` opened every fight at 0, which is wrong wherever a kit restores a gauge on entering combat. Denia's inherent Vestiges of Falsehood — "restore Dark Cores to 2 if she has fewer than 2" — means her Banish Stage 2 consumed **1** core where the game gives **3**, feeding her "+150% per Dark Core consumed" ×2.5 instead of ×5.5 (measured **2.20× understated at S0–S2, 3.40× at S3+**, on her single biggest cast). `RESOURCE_DEFS.start` is the level the FIGHT opens on and `chainOverrides` the per-chain edit: S3 raises her limit to 5 AND enhances the inherent to "restored to the max", so the two must move together — an S3 build opens 5/5 and its Intro grant overflows, while at S0 the same grant is needed. A CARRIED level OUTRANKS `start` (`startLevels ?? def.start ?? 0`), because `start` is what entering the FIGHT gives, not each segment: re-reading it per segment refills the gauge for free. Only the S3 branch is in the data (trigger row `1211700101`, cd 12.0, +100% of `SpecialEnergy2Max`); the base floor and the 3→5 bump appear nowhere in `gauge-income.json` — no `isCap` row exists for her — so those two numbers are the kit's, validated by the sim now reproducing the game's own pre-multiplied THREE-core row. ~~Modelled at fight start only: the kit's "once every 12s" also refills on a later swap-in, which the per-cast model has no trigger for.~~ It is a CYCLE — see the tick invariant below |
| A gauge tick is a CLOCK, and the gate withholds the EFFECT not the CLOCK | "This effect can be triggered once every 12s" is a rate limit on a passive that re-checks its condition, not a one-shot: `RESOURCE_DEFS.tick` fires at elapsed 0 and every `period` after, on **gameTime**, so it freezes during a Liberation animation like every other in-game timer. `refillTo` RAISES to a floor and never lowers (what "restore to 2 if she has fewer than 2" means); `amount` is the additive shape; `refillTo: 'cap'` names the gauge's own limit rather than repeating a number, so a chain node that raises the cap raises the floor with it. A FORM decides how far a firing refills, NOT whether it fires: `byState` varies the VALUE while the count of firings still advances, so a form that lowers the value cannot push the next firing later. ~~A `state` gate withholds the PAYOUT.~~ Read as a gate on Stagecraft Form, Denia's Breakdown Form branch paid NOTHING — her passive stops at 2 Dark Cores in Stagecraft and is UNLIMITED in Breakdown, one timer with two ceilings, while the CAP is 3 in both forms (maintainer, 2026-09-02; only the Stagecraft half is in the kit text and neither half is in `gauge-income.json`). A firing landing exactly on step i's start belongs to the step already IN PROGRESS, so it reads step i-1's states — the same rule that credits the t=0 firing before step 0 reads its level. The phase CARRIES across segments (`resourceEndTickPhases` -> `carryInResourceTicks`, threaded exactly like the levels), because a gauge tick belongs to the FIGHT: restarted per segment it re-fires its opening tick on every swap-in, which is free income. Counting FIRINGS (`floor(T / period) + 1`) rather than tracking a next-timestamp is what makes that carry exact with no "already fired at 0" special case. A caller with no `stepTimes` (rotation-graph legality) gets an INERT clock, never a guessed schedule. Denia's two 12s rows are the only wired case and her own rotation is 9.8s — SHORTER than one period — so her reference run exercises the t=0 firing alone and proves none of the cycle: the unit tests do. Measured: 1 pass identical, 3 passes +1.02%, and roster-wide 26 of 416 meta teams moved, all containing Denia, all UP, zero down, zero non-Denia teams touched |
| A chain node can CREATE the skill a rotation step names | Lupa S6: "Forte Circuit Dance With the Wolf is replaced with Dance With the Wolf: Climax". The game ships BOTH as damage rows (12070007001, 12070007002), so both resolve as ordinary skill keys and nothing stopped an S0 build from casting the S6 one — her own REFERENCE ROTATION does, inflating that step 1.35x and her whole rotation **8.13%**, in every meta team (`team-rank.js` builds members at chain 0). `CHAIN_LOCKED_SKILLS` + `resolveChainLockedRotation` SUBSTITUTE rather than drop, because the player presses the same input either way and the game decides which move comes out; dropping the step would understate by removing a cast that happens. The pair is reported as `chainLockedSubstitutions` rather than applied silently. A lock is only expressible when BOTH keys exist — Qiuyuan S3's Straw Cape in Drizzly Rain and Camellya S6's Perennial have NO rows at all, so those are missing-damage gaps (understating) and cannot be written as locks. Measured: 17 of 416 meta teams moved, all containing Lupa, all DOWN (-1.42%..-5.37%), zero non-Lupa teams touched |
| Gauge income is readable ON A CAST, and only there | ~~Gauge INCOME is NOT in the dumps and stays curated~~ — that held only for the four `data/bindata/` tables (Changli's 40 damage instances all read `SpecialEnergy 0`). The grant is not on the damage instance, it is on the CAST: `DT_SkillInfo` row → `SkillBuff`/`SkillStartBuff`/`SkillEndBuff` → `db_buff.GameAttributeID` ∈ `Proto_(Special)Energy*` (`data/gauge-income.json` ← `extract_gauge_income.py`, 50 resonators). Denia's three gauges reproduce her kit to the digit with no text parsing — cap 3/100/100 and +1/+25/+40. What it CANNOT see is income earned on a HIT: that lives in `db_PassiveSkill` (`DamageTrigger`, with its own CD) behind ExtraEffect chains, which is why Changli's Enflamement and Camellya's Crimson Bud show nothing in the cast lane and stay curated. The trigger lane is extracted but NOT wired — `rotation-resources.js` is per-cast by construction. Read the magnitude through `CalculationPolicy` (`ActiveBuff.ModifyStateAttribute`): **0** flat add, **1** scale base (`-10000` ⇒ zeroed), **2/4/9** fraction of `policy[1]`, **3** override (`0` ⇒ zeroed). Draining a gauge by its own full size is a flat `add` of `-cap`, NOT a `spendAll` shape — a reader that looks only for `spendAll` misses half the roster's spends |
| A Resonance MODE is not a STATE | A mode is a build-level toggle, locked for the fight (`build.resonanceMode` → `effect.mode` → `modeGateOk`). `STATE_DEFS` is for in-combat states a CAST enters. Modelling a mode as a state makes it switch on mid-rotation and ignore the build's own toggle — and the state name can only ever match one mode, so it fires in the other one too. A roster-wide test forbids a state named after a mode |
| A missing STATE is not one missing clause, and an aggregate is not a cause | A kit gated on a named state resolves OFF entirely without a `STATE_DEFS` entry, so the cost of not modelling one is EVERY clause that names it — and the total then looks like a single effect's worth. Galbrena's [Demon Hypostasis] was held back because enabling it raised her own damage **30.66%** in her geared meta team, which I read as "the Afterflame gauge must be sitting at its cap of 40" and refused to ship. INSTRUMENTED (a trace on `computeResourceTimeline` across every segment of that team's scoring run): the gauge peaks at **8 in 1,673 of 1,682 segments and 0 in the other 9**, never once higher — `clearedOnSwap` plus one member's own income cannot reach more. The inference was unfounded and the hold was withholding kit. DECOMPOSED by stripping each effect in turn, which is the measurement that should have come first: SN0.0's flat **+85% DMG Multiplier** on four named Forte moves is **+26.62%**, the Afterflame clause **+2.18%**, both +30.66%. 87% of the number had nothing to do with the gauge I was worried about. The ENTRY is derived, because her text never states it: her S1 node grants, "when casting Resonance Skill - Ascent of Malice", a buff "removed upon exiting Demon Hypostasis", and a cast whose grant dies with the state starts it. The Liberation is NOT an entry though it carries the 85% — its own node says "While in [Demon Hypostasis], cast [Seraphic Execution Stage 2] instead", presupposing the state. EXIT takes the kit's 50s rather than `persist`; the real limiter is Purging Flame depleting, which is unmodelled, so 50s is the longer bound and overstates where a fight ends the state early — it cannot bite today (her rotation is 12.16s and re-enters every pass). A clause stating BOTH a duration and a state ("for 14s while in [Demon Hypostasis]") keeps only the state half in the parser, measured as a non-difference: `stateBound`, a 14s window on the Liberation cast, and that window with no state gate all score her at 527,185 to the digit. Measured roster-wide: **7 of 315 meta teams moved, all 7 containing her, all up, zero without her** |
| The game may ship a per-resource multiplier as PRE-MULTIPLIED rows — read them as the answer key, not as the model | Denia's [Banish - Breakdown Form Stage 2] displays 56.34%, and `damageTable` also holds `12111052110/…120/…130/…140/…150` at exactly 2.5× / 4.0× / 5.5× / 7.0× / 8.5× of it — `base × (1 + 1.5N)` for N = 1..5 Dark Cores (five because S3 states "Denia now holds up to 5"). The skill's own row (`12110002005`) IS present and IS what the sim uses, so those variants are not needed to make the cast deal damage; attaching them as extra hits would double-count. They are the CHECK: `computeDamage` already composes `baseMult × (1 + multiplierUp)`, so a correct per-resource model must reproduce each variant exactly, and `tests/rotation-resources.test.mjs` asserts all five at every level of the curve |
| A "for each [X] CONSUMED" multiplier scopes ITSELF | The clause names no skill ("the DMG Multiplier of the attack"), so there is no name to bind — and none is needed. Its stack count reads what the STEP SPENDS (`computeResourceConsumption`, `stackTrigger.consumed`), which is 0 on every cast that spends nothing, so the effect is scoped by arithmetic and is exempt from the unscoped-`multiplierUp` drop. Two things make that safe and must stay true: the window is `thisCast` (with `persist` the effect is dark on the very cast it is for unless a sibling key fired first, then live on every step after), and an UNCURATED gauge consumes 0 rather than reading as unknown — `resourceConsumedAt` returns 0, never null, so a gauge the app does not model understates instead of paying on every cast. A clause scaling on the gauge merely HELD is NOT this shape and still needs a real scope |
| A gauge belongs to the CHARACTER, not to a segment | A team turn is simulated as several `simulateRotation` calls — the auto-injected Intro is its own segment — so a gauge earned in one and spent in another reads empty unless it is carried. `simulateRotation` takes `carryInResources` and returns `resourceEndLevels`; team-sim threads them through `sim.memberResources`, Intro → rotation → next pass, exactly as `memberFires` carries the trigger ledger. Denia earns her Dark Core on Intro and spends it on Banish Stage 2, so without the carry her multiplier is correct solo and silently zero in every team sim |
| A fight starts on a FULL Resonance meter, and a curated rotation is performed AS AUTHORED | Maintainer-directed 2026-08-14, modelled on the Tower of Adversity start state (`team-energy.js`, `team-sim.js`: "STARTS FULL"). Resonance Energy starts FULL (`baseStats.energyMax`) for every resonator; Concerto and Forte/SpecialEnergy start EMPTY; overcap is impossible (the gauge clamps at the cost, and the cost IS the meter). Generation spilled before the first Liberation is fine: a Liberation is placed for its BUFF WINDOW, not its energy efficiency. The engine may neither splice filler into a curated rotation nor drop a cast from it; a short gauge is a BUILD problem, so report the shortfall and the ER that fixes it (derived padding and gating are retired). Pass 1 is the WEAKEST pass (empty Concerto, no buffs ramped); the ER target binds from the SECOND pass. |

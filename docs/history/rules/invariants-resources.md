## A gauge tick is a CLOCK, and the gate withholds the EFFECT not the CLOCK

### 2026-10-07 — condensed to current truth

The row's full Detail text before condensation, verbatim (it holds the superseded and struck-through text, measurements, dated corrections and discovery narrative that left the row):

"This effect can be triggered once every 12s" is a rate limit on a passive that re-checks its condition, not a one-shot: `RESOURCE_DEFS.tick` fires at elapsed 0 and every `period` after, on **gameTime**, so it freezes during a Liberation animation like every other in-game timer. `refillTo` RAISES to a floor and never lowers (what "restore to 2 if she has fewer than 2" means); `amount` is the additive shape; `refillTo: 'cap'` names the gauge's own limit rather than repeating a number, so a chain node that raises the cap raises the floor with it. A FORM decides how far a firing refills, NOT whether it fires: `byState` varies the VALUE while the count of firings still advances, so a form that lowers the value cannot push the next firing later. ~~A `state` gate withholds the PAYOUT.~~ Read as a gate on Stagecraft Form, Denia's Breakdown Form branch paid NOTHING — her passive stops at 2 Dark Cores in Stagecraft and is UNLIMITED in Breakdown, one timer with two ceilings, while the CAP is 3 in both forms (maintainer, 2026-09-02; only the Stagecraft half is in the kit text and neither half is in `gauge-income.json`). A firing landing exactly on step i's start belongs to the step already IN PROGRESS, so it reads step i-1's states — the same rule that credits the t=0 firing before step 0 reads its level. The phase CARRIES across segments (`resourceEndTickPhases` -> `carryInResourceTicks`, threaded exactly like the levels), because a gauge tick belongs to the FIGHT: restarted per segment it re-fires its opening tick on every swap-in, which is free income. Counting FIRINGS (`floor(T / period) + 1`) rather than tracking a next-timestamp is what makes that carry exact with no "already fired at 0" special case. A caller with no `stepTimes` (rotation-graph legality) gets an INERT clock, never a guessed schedule. Denia's two 12s rows are the only wired case and her own rotation is 9.8s — SHORTER than one period — so her reference run exercises the t=0 firing alone and proves none of the cycle: the unit tests do. Measured: 1 pass identical, 3 passes +1.02%, and roster-wide 26 of 416 meta teams moved, all containing Denia, all UP, zero down, zero non-Denia teams touched

## Gauge income is readable ON A CAST, and only there

### 2026-10-07 — condensed to current truth

The row's full Detail text before condensation, verbatim (it holds the superseded and struck-through text, measurements, dated corrections and discovery narrative that left the row):

~~Gauge INCOME is NOT in the dumps and stays curated~~ — that held only for the four `data/bindata/` tables (Changli's 40 damage instances all read `SpecialEnergy 0`). The grant is not on the damage instance, it is on the CAST: `DT_SkillInfo` row → `SkillBuff`/`SkillStartBuff`/`SkillEndBuff` → `db_buff.GameAttributeID` ∈ `Proto_(Special)Energy*` (`data/gauge-income.json` ← `extract_gauge_income.py`, 50 resonators). Denia's three gauges reproduce her kit to the digit with no text parsing — cap 3/100/100 and +1/+25/+40. What it CANNOT see is income earned on a HIT: that lives in `db_PassiveSkill` (`DamageTrigger`, with its own CD) behind ExtraEffect chains, which is why Changli's Enflamement and Camellya's Crimson Bud show nothing in the cast lane and stay curated. The trigger lane is extracted but NOT wired — `rotation-resources.js` is per-cast by construction. Read the magnitude through `CalculationPolicy` (`ActiveBuff.ModifyStateAttribute`): **0** flat add, **1** scale base (`-10000` ⇒ zeroed), **2/4/9** fraction of `policy[1]`, **3** override (`0` ⇒ zeroed). Draining a gauge by its own full size is a flat `add` of `-cap`, NOT a `spendAll` shape — a reader that looks only for `spendAll` misses half the roster's spends
